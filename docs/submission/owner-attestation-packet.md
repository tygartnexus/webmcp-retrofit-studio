# Owner Attestation Packet

## Purpose and boundary

This packet collects facts and acknowledgements that cannot be established from source code, browser tests, public readback, or a general implementation approval. It is a submission-control checklist, not legal advice. The entrant should review the current [Official Rules](https://webmcp.devpost.com/rules/), [Devpost Terms of Service](https://info.devpost.com/legal/terms-of-service), [Devpost Privacy Policy](https://info.devpost.com/legal/privacy-policy), and [OpenAI supported-country list](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/supported-countries) before answering.

Do not place a birth date, street address, government identifier, financial account information, password, or recovery code in this repository or in an agent prompt. Devpost may collect information directly through its own authenticated forms.

## Entrant facts only the owner can supply

1. Entrant capacity: **Individual**, **Team**, or **Organization**.
2. Exact public entrant, team, or organization name.
3. Country and state/province/territory of residence or domicile, confirmation that it is supported and not excluded by the current rules, plus confirmation that every participating individual is at least the age of majority where they reside. A birth date is not requested here.
4. If entering as a Team: complete public member list, confirmation that every member is individually eligible and located in a supported, non-excluded jurisdiction, named representative, and confirmation that the representative is authorized to act and submit for the Team.
5. If entering as an Organization: confirmation that it legally existed and was organized or incorporated at entry in a supported, non-excluded jurisdiction, complete participating-member list, named representative, and confirmation that the representative is individually eligible and authorized to act and submit for the Organization.
6. Eligibility/conflict statement: disclose any Sponsor, Devpost, promotion-entity, judge, employment, agency, affiliate, immediate-family, household, or other relationship that could create an exclusion or real/apparent conflict; otherwise attest that none applies after reviewing Official Rules section 3.
7. Work-period classification:

   - confirm the entire project was newly created after August 25, 2026 at 11:00 AM Pacific Time; or
   - identify every pre-existing component and the meaningful WebMCP extension added during the submission period, with dated evidence.

8. Other challenge submissions: state **None**, or list them and explain why this project is unique and substantially different.
9. Financial or preferential support: state **None**, or disclose any Sponsor/Administrator funding, investment, development contract, project-specific commercial license, or preferential support before the end of the submission period.
10. Outside contributors or contractors: state **None**, or identify their contribution and the evidence that the entrant owns the resulting submission components.
